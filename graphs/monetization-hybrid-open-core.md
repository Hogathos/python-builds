# Monetization hybrid open core

Date: 2026-11-15
Status: decided, best practice assumed

## Problem
Iris had to pay for itself, without paywalling the verdict, and without building billing from scratch.

## Intention
Free local core forever. Subscription for cloud only. Enterprise for governance, audit, SSO, and compliance. Mars paid red team later.

## Options
Charge per verdict. Charge for the local app. Subscription for everything. Hybrid open core.

## Choice
Hybrid, open core, Obsidian model. Free local: Watch, Verify, Steer, Meter, in the exe, AGPL. Subscription: cloud sync, backup, long term history, sharing an evidence pack. Enterprise, custom: governance, audit, SSO, compliance, signed evidence packs, support. Mars later: paid red team.

## Rejected
Charge per verdict. Verdicts are not products. Do not charge per verdict.
Paywall the local core. The core is free, local, AGPL, forever.
Build billing yourself. Billing is Stripe, Paddle, or Lemon Squeezy. Cloudflare Monetization Gateway is an agent route under review, not enabled. Do not build billing from scratch.
